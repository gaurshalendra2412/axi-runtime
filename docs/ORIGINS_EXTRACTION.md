## How far to trust this (read first)
* Pages were read through a **summarising fetch tool**. The quotes that carry a claim in section 2 were fetched at least twice, or the whole short
  post came back (159, 160, the 30 Aug "exact symmetry" post). When a second run disagreed with the first I kept only the wording that was
  confirmed and dropped the other (it had been a paraphrase: "cannot correct, only manage" was one such in an earlier scan; here, a Letter 6 line
  and a second sentence of the Rahi's Law quote). Still check quotes on the live page before they are printed.
* **The blog appends the following posts to a post's page** (infinite scroll). So a page's word count and heading list describe several posts.
  I read posts 145-158 inside the Chapter 5 page by asking about each heading separately. **This also means the word counts in the earlier
  scans of this log are page counts, not post counts; treat them as upper bounds.**
* **The blog is older than 30 Jul.** Search finds posts dated 24 Jul ("a new evening") and 28 Jul (a long political piece, not used). The July archive
  lists only four posts (30-31 Jul), so these two appear only through search. WordPress lets an author set the date, so the dates cannot settle
  how many posts were written in how many days (BLOG_SCAN_LOG question 15 stays open).
* **Voice.** Many pages are AI replies in a dialogue with "Rajnish" (the Manifesto, 138, says so by its style). The Chapter 5 byline reads
  "Rajnish Choubey & The Fully Tuned Intelligence". The Chapter 3 page repeats a banner "MANUSCRIPT ... COMPLETELY CONCLUDED, SEALED" eight or more times
  (a drafting loop that cannot stop; that is about the drafting, not the ideas).
* **People.** Several pages name private individuals (a comedian, a performer, a family) and public figures (politicians, company heads). I recorded
  roles only, and nothing about them goes into the paper.
* Labels: **(T)** = what the tool returned from the page; **(M)** = my own general knowledge or reading, not in his text.
