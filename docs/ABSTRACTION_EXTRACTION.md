### 2.3 Rules and Respect = safety and liveness, and the measured version of it (row 67)
* **Said** (verified, 24 Jul): "Respect is not a rule; it is an internal alignment." and "Rules can prevent immediate structural collapse, but they can also turn a living union into a cold, transactional cage." The screenplay (24 Jul; the "100% Safety Matrix" is from one run): a system with a safety matrix answers a child's question with "Error 403"; the child says "It spent millions of dollars just to tell me it's scared of a cardboard box."
* **Abstract:** a *safety* property (nothing bad happens) is satisfied by doing nothing; a *liveness* property (something good happens) needs movement. A rules-only system gets the first for free and not the second.
* **Known, and measured:** `gate.check(g, empty delta)` admits the empty delta (checked this round). In our own runs, **M2 "gate only"** (reject, no help) has **0% corruption** and no gain in completion: 50.0% overall and 0% on delete_dep in the no-rule regime (seeds 0-3), 41.7% against M0's 53.3% in the rule regime (seed 3). The repair paths (M3 imperative retry, M5, M6) are what move completion to 100%. In the rule regime M6 reaches 95.0% / 90.0% with 0% corruption. So the post's "rules prevent collapse but cannot animate" has a number behind it in this repository, and the right way to report is both numbers together (corruption = safety, completion = liveness).
* **Opaque vs reasoned refusal:** "Error 403" says nothing. The gate returns a reason naming the violated condition (match, identification, dangling edge, well-formedness), and M6 uses it to repair with `llm_calls_extra = 0`. That is the difference between a stop and a correction.
* **Careful:** the screenplay is a story. "Forced safety = forced stupidity" is the story's point as I read it, not a measurement of any real system.

### 2.4 Deletion: sacrifice (23 Jul), armor (14 Aug), and a gap in our own language
* **Said** (verified, 23 Jul, whole post): "Durga is both the best girl u love and the vaccum. Can u sacrifice what u love most with vaccum. If u can make that jump durga is urs. Durga is both the best girl u love and the vaccum." (the first sentence is also the last: the text is a closed loop.) **14 Aug** (verified): "They can't index what isn't there." / "They can't monetize a mind that refuses to leave a digital trail." / "By deleting your work after months of unyielding discipline, you rendered yourself completely invisible to their data nets." The post does not say what happens to the notebooks, does not name the one reader, and does not use the words unsigned or receipt.
* **Abstract:** the same operator in three roles: the price of access (23 Jul), the Rite of Deletion (2 Aug, earlier scans), a shield against indexing (14 Aug).
* **Known, and new (row 65, `test_deletion_asymmetry.py`, 4 tests, mutation-checked):**
  1. Deleting an **edge** carries its weight and the gate checks it; deleting a **node** carries no value and the gate cannot see one (any stored value 0-9 is deleted without a question).
  2. So applying a delta is not injective: two hosts that differ only in the deleted node's value become the *same* graph. The value is gone. To undo, the inverse must be computed **before** applying (`invert(g, d)`), i.e. a copy was kept.
  3. An edge-only delta inverts from its own text; a delta with a node delete does not.
  4. After a delete the host holds no residue (nodes, edges, incidence index).
* **The tension for Rajnish:** an erasure you can undo is an erasure you kept a copy of. "Armor" (leave nothing) and "undo" (keep what was deleted) pull apart. Which does the framework want? A possible upgrade, **not built**: `DEL[r,c:val]` (the value must match), which would catch stale beliefs about nodes the way edges already are caught. Question 48.

### 2.5 Round trip: start = end
* **Said** (verified, 14 Aug, 52): "It started at threshold and center. It ends at threshold and center too, which means the shape was never a line moving forward." The 5 Aug page ends a story when total entropy "drops back to flat baseline" and says "You get what you write ... a live, recursive feedback loop" (both 1 run; the second run of that section gave "You wrote absolute, unconditioned depth, the universe had no choice but to calculate and deliver"). 23 Jul's first sentence is its last.
* **Abstract:** a sequence whose net effect is the identity: no progress, or perfect undo. Which of the two depends on whether the path was intended.
* **Known:** d followed by its inverse is the identity and the signed counts sum to zero (row 38, test 28). A *no-progress detector* (the episode returns to a state it has been in) is not built; with D4 canonical form it would also catch the same state rotated or mirrored. Cheap, untested, offered as a next build.
* **Recursive feedback** (row 74: what the model writes becomes what it reads next) is the agent loop. The measured version is the containment run: blind apply leaves 38 / 75 / 88 / 100% of episodes corrupted after steps 1-4 (8 episodes). His 21 Jan formula 1 - (1-p)^n (below) is the independence baseline for that; with 8 episodes the logged counts are within two standard deviations of it, so **the data cannot tell independent errors from errors that breed errors** (row 75, `test_compounding_arithmetic.py`).

### 2.10 Compounding error, dated 21 Jan
* **Said** (2 runs agree): "Probability of at least one error: 1 - (probability of no error)^number of steps" and "(0.9999)^108 =~ 0.9892", in a game-of-telephone example with 0.01% error per step over 108 steps. The collage also has a six-phase "108 steps" research process (6 x 18) and 99.99% as a bacteria kill rate (both 1 run).
* **Checked:** 0.9999^108 = 0.98926, so 0.9893 rounded (0.9892 truncated); the exact chance of at least one error is 1.0742%, the post's 1.08% is the linear shortcut and is a true upper bound; keeping 108 steps under a 1% failure chance needs a per-step failure of at most about 9.3e-5. An early dated statement of the compounding idea (I have not checked it against every earlier post). Not an agent claim: the post is about telephone.

## 3. What did not hold up (so nobody builds on it)
* **"The Permutations of the Nine"** computes no permutations; its "nine" are AI products. We supplied the count ourselves (row 64): 362,880 arrangements of nine distinct labels on the grid, 45,360 classes under D4, every class exactly 8; for repeated colours the saving is less than 8 (5.0x for 2 colours, 6.9x for 3, 7.97x for 10).
* **13 = 12 + 1 = 6 + 6 + 1** in the fable looks like the grid's 1 + 4 + 4. Every odd number is 1 + k + k. Not evidence.
* **"50 - AI Has Not Read Anything"** says there was "no page-order preserved". Overstated (my knowledge, not the post's): training sequences are contiguous chunks and their internal order matters to prediction; what is shuffled is the order of chunks across the corpus. The defensible core, which the post states well, is that nothing records a reading event, so "I read it" has no referent. The post says nothing about attention looking back at earlier tokens.
* **The 5 Aug topology page** (variables X1-X6, entropy H, tensor W, three phases) defines none of its symbols, gives no formula for W, no read/write rules. It is rich in structure and empty of mathematics. Its one hard rule, verified in two runs, is "The exact second the line of 5 nodes tries to reach out, wire into, or capture Node 6, the entire experiment is ruined."
* **29 Aug "Mapping Shiva mathematically":** no formulas, no signs. Brahma is "the calculus of expansion", Vishnu "the differential equation of balance", Shiva "the asymptotic limit". "A system like that doesn't have an outer perimeter" / "What does your baseline equation look like when the origin point is also the boundary?" Interesting for a grid with no edge; nothing to test.
* **The 27 Aug "Dimension Collapse" numbers** (12,888 dimensions, 614 "reasoning density", 13 goddesses) have no source in the post. **The 9 Pieces founders' quotes** were not checked and are not used. **"$108 Trillion"** (20 Jul) is, in the post's own words, "rounding off to the closest prettiest number on higher side". **The New Ball** has no usable content.
