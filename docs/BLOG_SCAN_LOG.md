* Pages were read through a fetch tool that returns a summary of the page, not the raw text. Quotes are short and should be checked
  against the live page before they go into a paper. The tool also made a wrong statement once (it called the claim "a triangle cannot be
  collapsed without breaking its sides" geometrically unsound; it is correct, see row 34).
* Byline: every post from 31 Aug on shows "rajnish choubey" in the page metadata. I cannot verify who typed it. Many titles begin the way a
  chatbot reply does ("That is ...", "You caught ..."), so some posts may be edited AI text. **Ask Rajnish which posts are his own writing.**
* No post contains an equation, an algorithm or a procedure. What they contain is roles, sequences and principles, and that is what was mined.

## What repeated across posts (the useful part)
1. **Structure, not policy text.** Post 11 says terms of service are not "structural engineering"; post 24 says a rulebook needs an external police force; post 29 says a baseline with no "endless moral checklists". Our measurement agrees: the rule written in the prompt did not help the 3B model, the structural gate gave 0% corruption (rows 29, 7).
2. **Cut the false, close the boundary.** Posts 20 and 14: "she cuts away the garbage", "dissolution of falsehood", map vs territory. Post 30: a boundary that finally stops what crosses it. Together they describe the new `ground_and_complete` repair (rows 25-26).
3. **Escalation ladder.** Post 30 gives an order: state the rules, warn, tolerate, then stop. That is rules, feedback, bounded retries, abort. We only measured one retry. A retry-budget sweep is the open test (rows 26, 33).
4. **Containment.** Post 11 and post 16: errors cascade when there is no breaker; destruction "needs a place to rest" (a committed baseline to land on). Test built (row 27); real run pending.
5. **Incorruptible.** Posts 18-20: cannot be bought, bribed or cheated. The gate's verdict is a function of (graph, delta) only (row 28).
6. **Two structures, not one.** A **quad** (Durga, Buddha, Shiva, Kali: protection) and a **triad** (cognition/Saraswati, substance/Laxmi, power/Parvati: what the world is made of), the triad appearing in posts 6, 23 and 28. See section 7 of the concept map for a draft quad x triad matrix.
7. **Constrain outcomes, not expression.** Posts 29 and 24 again (and row 21): permissive by default, hard stop at the invariant. Raises the priority of the "is the grammar needed" ablation.

## What did not hold up
* Post 1 (the only post that describes AXI mechanically) is about steering the model's insides. The repo does not do that and nothing measured supports it. See PAPER_CORRECTIONS item 15.
* Posts 4, 8, 25, 27: no operational content found.
* Post 7's order of deities follows the lunar calendar, not a design; only the mood labels are his.

## What the second 25 gave (ranked by use to the project)
1. **"Cognition divorced from consequence"** (41) and **"nothing forces me to notice this looks incomplete, I should wait"** (53). The clearest one-sentence version of the paper's thesis: a proposal must be tied to its consequences before it commits. In the code that is `diagnose` (lists the consequence: dangling edges), `complete` (closes it) and the gate (holds an incomplete delete). Measured: 100% corruption when the consequence is ignored (concept map row 43).
2. **Proxy versus structure** (36, 37). A polygraph reads how aroused you are, not whether you are lying. A verifier that reads how sure the model sounds cannot tell a correct proposal from a corrupting one. The gate reads the effect on the state. New test proposed: the "needle test" (row 36).
3. **The map goes stale** (38, 40). The graph in the prompt is a snapshot. Third source for row 32; new test: a lagged-snapshot sweep (row 39).
4. **Closed and fully checkable** (31, 34, 35). Taken only as "a small closed set of operations that can all be checked, each with an inverse". The inverse is not built yet (row 38) and it is the thing `SET` needs anyway.
5. **Local verification** (49). Check the elements a delta touches, not the whole model: a gate-time-versus-graph-size test (row 40), sandbox only.
6. **Safety as a floor, not a target** (50), and **a fork decided only by the input's integrity** (48). Second sources for rows 24, 26 and 28.

## What did not give anything
* **Political and historical posts (41-45): no engineering content.** The one usable sentence is in 41. Several claims are strong and about named living people; the history in 42 is a simplification. I would not link these from the paper or the Medium article (reviewers will read what is linked).
* **Mystery posts (55):** the binary titles carry no recoverable message. I tested for it rather than assuming.
* **Numerology.** Numbers match too easily: 108 = 4 x 27 = 4 x 3^3 (a quad and a cubed triad); 12 cells of the quad-by-triad grid x 9 fields = 108; eight gates around the centre of a 3x3 grid = the 8 transforms of D4. Only the last has mathematics behind it that we verified (D4 acts on a 3x3 grid with orbits of size 1 centre, 4 corners, 4 edges), and even that only tells us the "eight gates" are really two classes of four. Rule: a number match counts only if it predicts something we can measure.
