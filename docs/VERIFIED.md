# What is verified, what is not (honest status)

A one-page summary of latency, drift, cost, what is not shown and where the tensor-level roadmap stands: [`WHERE_THIS_STANDS.md`](WHERE_THIS_STANDS.md) (every figure on it is checked against the page it names by `tests/test_where_this_stands.py`, test 121).

## Ticks: the two core problems, measured here (8 Oct 2026)
| Problem the industry talks about | Tick | What was measured (Qwen2.5-7B, 4-bit, Colab T4, synthetic service graphs; repeated on Qwen2.5-14B on 9 Oct) | Still open before it is a general tick |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Latency**: what checking costs, and what a growing context costs | **ticked for this setup** | gate check plus diagnosis a few microseconds against 1 to 2.5 s per model call; M2, M5 and M6 add no model call, a retry adds one (2 to 5 times the time); constrained decoding no measurable overhead; a kept chat made each step 5.1 times slower by step 19, fresh exact state every step stayed flat; at 14B (9 Oct) the check was still about 13 microseconds against 3.2 to 3.9 s per call, and one request took 2.3 times (no rule) and 1.7 times (rule) the 7B's time (`LATENCY_7B.md`, `LONG_HORIZON_TEST.md`, `SCALE_TEST_14B.md`) | other families; sampling; cache-reusing engines; contexts above about 5,500 tokens (the T4 ran out of memory); Rust crates not timed |
| **Drift**: errors that compound over many steps | **ticked for this setup, including unrequested changes, when the request names its targets by coordinate** | over 30 steps blind apply was corrupted in 12 of 12 episodes, the gate in 0 of 360 steps, gate plus M6 exactly on the intended state on 358 of 360 steps (no rule); with the rule stated M6 was exact on only 55 % of steps because of legal changes nobody asked for (`LONG_HORIZON_TEST.md`). Then the fix, run on fresh seeds (`SCOPE_TEST.md`): named scope took the rule-stated prompt from 118 to 134 correct graphs of 140, harmed none, and over 30 steps held the intended state on 98.3 % of steps against 73.1 % for M6; with no rule it changed nothing (98.8 % both). Repeated on Qwen2.5-14B (9 Oct, `SCALE_TEST_14B.md`): gate, M6 and scope_m7 corrupted nothing in 840 single requests and 600 long steps; scope_m7 exact on 180 of 180 steps (rule stated) and 120 of 120 (no rule); the raw model still corrupted the graph on 24 of 140 requests (no rule) and in all 10 long episodes, so the gain is smaller (M6 over blind apply 24 tasks, 7B 36), not gone | requests written in ordinary words (the filter reads coordinates; with none it is exactly M6); implicit-target requests such as "remove every edge with weight w" (11 of 280 outputs wrong, all of that kind, the filter cannot help; 11 of 280 again at 14B); the two failure modes written down before the run never occurred, so they are untested; other families, sizes above 14B, frontier models; sampling; episodes longer than 30 steps; real (not synthetic) tasks; graphs with a service of more than four connections (the repair keeps such a service instead of deleting it; every task so far deletes services with at most three connections: `tests/test_hub_delete.py`) |
