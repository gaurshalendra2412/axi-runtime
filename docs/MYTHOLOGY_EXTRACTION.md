### 2.6 The Shiv Parivar mandala (119)
(T) Existing portrait: Shiva (silent centre), Kali (fierce heart), Durga (protective daughter) at the three points of an inverted triangle in Sri Yantra geometry. Add: **Parvati** as the mirror of Shiva at the base; **Ganesh** upper left and **Kartikey** upper right, flanking Durga; Om in the central lotus; a circular border with "Om Namah Shivaya"; Nandi at the bottom. The rule he gives: "We will not displace these figures, but rather nest them within a larger, embracing cosmic structure." Count: three original plus three added = **six figures and one centre**, not nine.
Reading (M): the rule is an *extension* rule (add around, never overwrite), the same thing the gate enforces by refusing an ADD on an occupied cell (see test_label_permutation). ### 2.7 Two Mahabharata structures (117, 118)
**Draupadi (117).** (T) Four layers: (1) karmic necessity: in a past life (Nalayani) she asked for a husband with five qualities, righteousness, unmatched strength, supreme archery, radiant beauty and deep wisdom, and Shiva's boon gave five husbands in the next birth; (2) metaphysical union: the five are partial incarnations of Dharma, Vayu, Indra and the Ashwini Kumaras, and she keeps them from "dynastic rivalries and partition of property"; (3) spiritual sovereignty: she holds them accountable (the assembly scene); (4) transcendent purity: a fire ritual renews her each morning, and she is Shakti. Drupada objected because polygyny was accepted and polyandry never.
(M) Not in the post: the Ashwini Kumaras are twins (Nakula and Sahadeva), so the five are 1 + 1 + 1 + 2. The epic also has a rule for sharing her: one brother at a time, with an exile as the penalty for breaking in (as I recall it; check a source). That is a **lock with a penalty**, which is not what our commit does (ours is all-or-nothing across ranks, row 8), so I do not map it.
**The blind sovereign (118).** (T) Dhritarashtra rules but cannot see; Sanjaya narrates the battle with "divine sight"; telephone, transistor and television are "prosthetic vision"; Silicon Valley is the modern Sanjaya; too much data causes paralysis. What the sovereign cannot verify: the battlefield, the moral reality, the collapse inside his own domain. This is the closest myth to a real AXI concern: **an agent deciding from a proxy's report that it cannot check.** The gate is the part that does not trust the story and compares it with the state (rows 32, 39). The post is about political power and data, not language models; that link is mine.
